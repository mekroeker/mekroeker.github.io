I am a postdoctoral fellow in the Insitute for Discrete Mathematics and Algebra at TU Freiberg, working under the mentorship of Johannes Carmesin. Prior to this, I completed my PhD in the Department of Combinatorics and Optimization at the University of Waterloo, where I was supervised by Jim Geelen.

I am interested in structural and extremal combinatorics, especially in the settings of matroid theory, discrete geometry and graph theory.

I am currently co-organizing the Discrete Mathematics and Algebra Seminar at TU Freiberg. Please consult our webpage for the upcoming schedule of speakers.
